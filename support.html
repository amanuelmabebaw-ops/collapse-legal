Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play Collapse?

Tap a number to remove it along with the operator next to it. The remaining expression updates instantly. Your goal is to reach the target number shown at the top of the puzzle. Compete mode runs five timed puzzles; Daily and Training are unlimited.

I purchased Collapse Pro but my devices don't show it.

In the app, open Settings and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure each device is signed into the same Apple ID you used to buy Pro. If the issue persists, email us with your Apple ID region (e.g. United States) and we'll investigate.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through Apple, not through us. On your iPhone:

  • Open the Settings app.
  • Tap your name at the top.
  • Tap Subscriptions.
  • Tap Collapse, then Cancel Subscription.

You'll keep Pro access until the end of your current billing period. No refund is issued automatically, but Apple offers refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my account and data?

Email us at amanuelabebaw@gmail.com from the device's email account, and we'll remove your gameplay records and anonymous account from our servers within 7 days.

My daily challenge is stuck or the streak reset incorrectly.

Streak boundaries follow your device's local timezone. If the streak still looks wrong, force-close Collapse and reopen it — the app refreshes from the server on launch. If the issue continues, email us with a screenshot.

I'm seeing too many ads / I want fewer ads.

Free play shows a short interstitial ad after every few Compete runs. Subscribing to Collapse Pro removes all ads entirely — that's the main thing Pro buys you, along with harder puzzle variants.

Is my data private?

Yes. We collect as little as possible — your account is anonymous unless you set a display name, and we never see your real identity, email, or payment info. Full details are in our Privacy Policy.
